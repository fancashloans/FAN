▼ Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Personal identification information (name, ID number, contact details)
  • Financial information (income, employment details, bank account information)
  • Documentation (ID copies, proof of residence, payslips)
  • Loan application and repayment history

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and evaluate your loan applications
  • Verify your identity and conduct credit checks
  • Communicate with you about your loan and account
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Improve our services and customer experience

3. Information Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. All sensitive data is encrypted and stored securely.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with:

  • Credit bureaus for credit assessment purposes
  • Service providers who assist in our operations
  • Legal authorities when required by law
  • Financial institutions for payment processing

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You may also object to certain processing activities. Contact us to exercise these rights.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about our privacy practices, please contact us at privacy@fancashloans.co.za

▼ Responsible Lending Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

Our Commitment

FAN CASH LOANS is committed to responsible lending practices that protect our customers and promote financial well-being. We believe in transparent, fair, and ethical lending.

Key Principles

1. Affordability Assessment

Before approving any loan, we conduct a thorough affordability assessment to ensure you can comfortably repay the loan without financial hardship. This includes:

  • Reviewing your income and expenses
  • Checking your existing debt obligations
  • Assessing your credit history
  • Ensuring the loan fits within your budget

2. Transparent Pricing

We provide clear, upfront information about all loan costs:

  • Fixed 5% monthly interest rate
  • No hidden fees or charges
  • Clear breakdown of total repayment amount
  • Written loan agreement with all terms clearly stated

3. Fair Treatment

We treat all customers fairly and with respect:

  • No discrimination based on race, gender, age, or other protected characteristics
  • Clear communication in language you understand
  • Respectful collection practices
  • Support for customers experiencing financial difficulty

4. Customer Support

We provide ongoing support throughout your loan journey:

  • Clear explanation of loan terms before you sign
  • Regular communication about your account
  • Assistance if you're struggling with repayments
  • Financial education resources

5. Compliance

We fully comply with:

  • The National Credit Act (NCA)
  • National Credit Regulator (NCR) regulations
  • Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)
  • All other applicable South African laws

If You're Struggling

If you're having difficulty making repayments, please contact us immediately. We're here to help and can discuss options such as:

  • Payment plan adjustments
  • Temporary payment relief
  • Debt counseling referrals
  • Financial advice and support
